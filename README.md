Minecraft advancements. Super fun, I know. This mod tries to give you a more tangible sense of accomplishment, by capturing all advancements from players on the server and displaying them on a Timeline with some extra "pezazz". *jazz fingers*🕺

It should be easy for server admins to add custom advancements and how they affect the Timeline's visuals, as well as adding custom events called "Notes" for everyone to read.

The **Timeline** mod tries to enhance roleplaying with a date system, as a simple "day counter" is too ubiquitous for players to immerse themselves in the adventure and story that unfolds.

!["Features" text banner](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_61fea4e2-116a-407d-ba3b-38bf40f4c559.png)

- **Date system**
    - Completely configurable date system that shows a daily toast to players with the current day. For example: *"Day 1 of Shadowmere, 420 After Notch"*

![Showing the "Date system" toast](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_fcaab6e0-21d8-4f85-9685-756b101af6c8.png)

- **Timeline GUI**
    - All advancements (including ones from mods and ones you add yourself) are recorded into the "Chronicle". You'll see a timeline with "nodes" on them, showing what people have accomplished around that time.

![Showing the main feature, the "Timeline"](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1454051/description_4f9ae7a1-8ae9-445b-9907-436e5f551129.png)

- **Hall of Fame**
    - Keeps track of how many Advancements people have accomplished, including whether they were "world-first". It's about time you are acknowledged for your accolades!

![Showing the "Hall of Fame" feature](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1454051/description_046e7481-087e-4e98-ac23-654350fe4b90.png)

- **Highly configurable**
    - Everything you see can be configured: how many months, names of each month, what is shown on the timeline.. You name it! And, all configuration is hotloaded. Meaning, whatever you change immediately goes into effect for all players.
- **Localization**
    - **Timeline** covers an extensive range of other languages: 27, to be exact! And I'm always willing to add more. Just reach out to me on Discord or via the comments and I'll get to it.
